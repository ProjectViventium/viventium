# Viventium V0.5

> **Status:** proposed product, architecture, and brand package under product-owner review. This
> directory contains design proposals only; it does not claim a V0.5 runtime exists.

V0.5 repositions Viventium as a local, user-owned personal continuity system around a person's
portable Life and Brain Pack. Codex, Claude, ChatGPT, GlassHive, LibreChat, and future local agents
remain capable but replaceable execution bodies or compatibility substrates.

## Review gate

The high-level architecture, quiet-graphite visual direction, brand tokens, and four-destination
product model remain proposals until the product owner explicitly accepts them. Do not promote them
into implementation or describe them as locked before that decision is recorded here.

## Boundary

Current shipped truth remains in the repository's numbered requirements, architecture, runtime,
and QA documentation. V0.5 slices must be promoted into those owners before implementation.
