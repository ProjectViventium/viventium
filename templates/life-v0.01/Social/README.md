# Social

Keep source-linked social profiles, publications, activity, relationships, comments, and media
references here. Create provider-specific children only for sources the person actually connects.
The originating service remains authoritative; Life stores governed projections, summaries,
evidence links, provenance, and sync state rather than an indiscriminate raw mirror.

Material about a person may also inform `People/`; project-related evidence may inform `Projects/`;
durable conclusions belong in the relevant canonical area with a link back to the social source.
