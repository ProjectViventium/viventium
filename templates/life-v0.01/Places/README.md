# Places

Keep user-approved places, location history, travel context, and geographic evidence here with
explicit sensitivity, precision, retention, and source rules. A place is part of the person's world;
photo or video metadata may support it but does not make it Media.

Link scheduled visits to `Calendar/`, lived experiences to `Events/`, people to `People/`, and
related photos or video to `Media/` without duplicating their authoritative evidence.
