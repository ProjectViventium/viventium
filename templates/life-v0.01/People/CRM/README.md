# CRM View

A user-owned relationship view: people, organizations, context, commitments, follow-ups, and
source-linked history. Contact and message providers remain authoritative. Do not duplicate entire
address books or inboxes here; maintain a useful, correctable pane over them.

Keep private deployments free to choose their own fields. At minimum, distinguish facts, the user's
notes, inferred relationship context, commitments, last verified state, and the source of each
claim.
