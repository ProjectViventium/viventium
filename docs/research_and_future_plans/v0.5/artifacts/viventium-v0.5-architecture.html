  • Energy · Mood · Drive

Reaction

The explainable change between baseline and Current, linked to what caused it and how long it should matter.

  • Affects attention and initiative
  • Affects caution and warmth
  • Affects exploration and expression

Feelings must cause behavior. If they do not change attention, initiative, caution, warmth, exploration, or voice expression, they are decoration—not cognition.

  • Constraints and success condition

Mutual context, not an MCP black box

  1. Create one mission identity and bounded workspace.
  2. Bootstrap navigation and delivery rules.
  3. Send the complete visible authorized context bundle.
  4. Exchange attributable questions, approvals, cancellations, and context deltas.
  5. Receive deliverables, evidence, status, result digest, and receipt.

T

Trust and runtime

Local-first is not a magic privacy claim. Aggregating a life increases breach impact, so permission, encryption, provenance, recovery, and deletion are architecture—not settings copy.

01Least privilegeRead, write, listen, presence, processing, and surfacing are separate permissions.
02Provenance and freshnessEvery consequential personal claim can show where it came from and whether the source was available.
03Human authorityPause, cancel, approve, correct, export, delete, and recover remain visible.
04Device threat modelProduction requires encryption at rest, device-loss response, protected backups, and verified recovery.
05No silent magicEmpty, denied, stale, failed, unavailable, and successful are different states.

Local runtime state
stays outside the portable self

Life and Brain Pack must remain inspectable and portable. Operational machinery remains derived, protected, replaceable, or rebuildable.

credentials · provider sessions · databases · recall indexes · queues · caches · logs · Current Feelings · active missions · scheduler state · run ledgers

LibreChat’s exact role: compatibility substrate and operator fallback during migration—not the V0.5 product hierarchy.

Path of least resistance.

  1. Keep the working V0.4 services. Voice, Telegram, Feelings, memory/recall, scheduling, cortices, GlassHive, and LibreChat continue behind stable local contracts.
  2. Add the thin Viventium shell. MIND, CONNECT, CHARACTER, and AUTOMATIONS become the clear control and visibility plane.
  3. Establish portable custody. Life and Brain Pack become user-owned formats, not a stripped copy of LibreChat.
  4. Use native AI bodies. Pass bounded context and permission in; receive results, evidence, deltas, and receipts out.
  5. Prove one full loop. A few sources → useful context → one coherent MIND → seamless mission → useful overnight insight → visible correction.
  6. Extract only what earns extraction. Move a capability out of LibreChat only when portability, reliability, or user experience materially improves.
