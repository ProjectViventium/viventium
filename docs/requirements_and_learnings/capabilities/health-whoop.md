# Health and WHOOP

## User promise

An owner may connect complete authorized WHOOP evidence for private health context without turning
raw health data into broad memory, diagnosis, or automatic chat injection.

## Authorized data

- **DATA-001 / DATA-009:** When authorized, ingest all six official WHOOP data families with complete
  pagination, preserved relationships and units, and enough structure for authorized Life
  correlation.

## Privacy

- **DATA-002:** Keep official ZIP and manual-import boundaries explicit. Project only metadata needed
  for Life context; do not copy raw private health data into broad context.

## Operation

- **DATA-004 / DATA-007:** Onboarding, recurring acquisition, and useful authorized analysis are automatic,
  durable, recoverable, and easy. Degraded access has one clear recovery action.

## Onboarding

- **DATA-008:** A supported owner can connect WHOOP through a generic minimal-click flow that uses an
  authorized official API, export, or manual path and reports a typed blocker when none works.

## Boundaries

- Acquisition, interpretation, memory, and user surfacing remain separate.
- Health data is evidence, not diagnosis. No routine optimizes for engagement or injects ungrounded
  health warnings.
- Provider secrets, raw records, exports, screenshots, and derived private context stay outside the
  public repository.
