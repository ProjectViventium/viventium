existing prompt editor API. Companionship and creative expression do not require new external

silent. Current context, Feelings, newer user boundaries and existing action permissions remain

receipt. Production intentionally suppresses `{NTA}` from that insight. The runner now recovers
only a strict silent marker from the exact completed native result, after route/source/request
verification, and passes it to semantic review. This changes QA evidence handling, not product
silence or delivery behavior.
