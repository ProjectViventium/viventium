## Findings

The affected software paths passed: original-context handoff, independent work and steering,
selected uploads, real browser actions, files, retained-result recovery, Telegram, active browser
Voice, hangup, reload and saved settings. Each result remains tied to its original author and source.

Physical iPhone speaker quality and microphone interruption remain separate hardware gates. The
model can still miss a saved writing preference; the tested clarification did not improve it and
was rejected. Full foreground multi-channel saturation was not tested. Live replies take several
seconds; the frozen-frame first-text median is not an end-to-end promise. No shipped component
commit or release parity is claimed.
