## Release rule

The availability flag remains false and the feature is not called complete while any applicable case
is `NOT RUN`, `FAIL`, `PARTIAL`, or `BLOCKED`. Unit/mocked tests support but do not replace the real
Telegram, browser, voice, scheduler, native harness, restart, and installed-runtime cases above.
