# Historical QA Review Criteria — 2026-05-17

This is a public summary of the dated review criteria. The exact reviewer brief and raw source
material are retained privately. The [dated repair report](2026-05-17-local-runtime-and-qa-repair.md)
owns the recorded evidence; current work follows the [QA operating contract](../../README.md).

The review checked:

- Traceability from requirements and natural user journeys to QA cases, named test owners and
  reproducible evidence.
- Whether source prompts, configured tools and generated behavior agreed within the repair scope.
- Preservation of unrelated local work, access boundaries and private source material.
- Clear separation of automated source checks, installed artifacts and real user-path evidence.
- Simple use of existing mechanisms, with precise findings and unresolved acceptance gaps instead
  of added process or unsupported completion claims.

These historical criteria do not establish acceptance of a later build or configuration.
