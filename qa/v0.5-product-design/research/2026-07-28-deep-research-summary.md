# V0.5 Interaction Research Summary

Gemini Pro Extended Deep Research was used as an independent evidence-discovery layer after the
Viventium requirements and provisional design were established. Its report remained open for owner
inspection. Recommendations were validated against first-party guidance and local product truth
before use.

## Conclusions used

- Intelligent setup should discover first, explain the boundary plainly, allow cancellation, and
  require review before enabling accounts.
- Browser/computer-use capability does not remove operating-system privacy or service authorization;
  the interface must not imply that discovery grants access.
- Multi-account identity belongs visibly under each service.
- Emotional history needs a readable text alternative to visual traces.
- Prompt variables need keyboard-accessible suggestions and one canonical representation.
- A persistent voice surface should remain compact, interruptible, and secondary to the current job.
- A lightweight desktop shell should prefer native/local primitives and narrow capabilities.

## Limits

This research did not validate Viventium runtime behavior. Some secondary sources in the generated
report were discarded; only recommendations that survived first-party and local-source checking
were adopted.
