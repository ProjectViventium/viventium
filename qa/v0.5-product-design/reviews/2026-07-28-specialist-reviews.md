# V0.5 Living Ledger — Fresh-Context Specialist Reviews

> **Scope:** public-safe review summaries. These are decision inputs, not owner approval and not
> runtime evidence.

## Visual and UI quality review

The reviewer received the V0.5 goals and current artifact without participating in its construction.

Findings carried into R2:

- remove the Here/Telegram switch and treat message origin as quiet provenance;
- make the recent Emotion Trail persist beside the conversation in MIND;
- put Find everything first, show cancellable progress, then present named accounts for review;
- make multiple accounts child rows rather than duplicate service cards;
- expand an Automation's cycle, instruction, delivery, and history in place;
- keep the full voice dock in MIND but compact it on working pages;
- reduce tiny labels and preserve the flat living-ledger composition.

## Interaction and current-practice research review

The reviewer challenged setup, consent, multi-account behavior, voice continuity, emotional
history, accessibility, and implementation weight.

Findings carried into R2:

- use **Delegate → Review → Live** for intelligent setup;
- one-click means one-click safe discovery, never silent authorization;
- account identities remain individually controllable under a service;
- every emotional history distinguishes recorded changes from invented continuous sampling;
- Automations is a lightweight view of one canonical Prompt Workbench object;
- `{{` suggestions follow an accessible combobox pattern;
- the voice control remains continuously available without becoming a fifth page;
- use native typography, semantic controls, SVG, and small local code instead of a dashboard,
  editor, or chart framework.

## Viventium source-of-truth accuracy review

The reviewer compared the artifact to the current Feelings kernel, live component source, scheduled
prompt surface, and V0.5 product documents.

Findings carried into R2:

- use the exact `FeelingBandDefinition` order, poles, words, Nature values, and return settings;
- keep typed model reactions at the canonical `3/8/15` movement strengths;
- distinguish sample state, manual changes, and actual reactions;
- first-run Feelings begins off;
- use Current/Natural, Now/Nature, Felt, and How quickly it returns in the places their meanings are
  clear; keep lane and half-life as internal language;
- use the real scheduled-prompt `{{` variable registry;
- place GlassHive under Workers and make it the capable discovery/delegation worker, not a protocol.

## Limits

These reviews were analytical. The independent browser QA and later Claude adversarial pass found
visible and behavioral defects the first reviews missed. Those escaped findings are recorded in the
R2 QA report so the review record remains honest.

## Living Mind R3 correction review

The same three independent perspectives reviewed the corrected R3 tree after implementation and
real-browser QA. Their first R3 passes found substantive issues rather than rubber-stamping the
artifact: voice-presence overlap, offscreen mobile keyboard focus, incomplete Cortex configuration,
non-persisted personal instructions, incomplete erase behavior, capsule edge cases, untyped or
misclassified visible movement, and misleading sample health wording.

Those findings became regression cases and were corrected. The final current-tree verdicts were:

|---|---|---|
| Visual and UI quality | PASS | 0 / 0 / 0 |
| Interaction and accessibility | PASS | 0 / 0 / 0 |

The project-accuracy reviewer independently confirmed the complete R3 browser suite green after its
final focus-race correction. Review findings remain supporting evidence; they do not convert this
