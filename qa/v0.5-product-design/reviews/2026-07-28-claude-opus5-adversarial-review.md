The reviewer inspected the current source, Viventium Feelings implementation, screenshots, QA
script, and live local artifact. Its strongest finding was that the first R2 trail technically

## Verified findings

- Per-emotion trails were visually flattened, used array position instead of elapsed time, put
  intensity pole labels under a time axis, and did not record manual changes.
- Find everything showed unlabeled fabricated results over already-connected sample accounts;
  checkbox selection did not drive the outcome, and connections did not persist through reload.
- The compact presence dock still covered central working content.
- Automation next-run copy did not update with cycle/time; rows were keyboard-dead; delivery choices
  duplicated; History was only a toast.
- Faint text failed contrast, mobile hid three of five Feeling levels behind an unmarked horizontal
  scroll, the Feelings master switch was cosmetic, and MIND retained stale Inner state.
- The QA report overstated its light-theme width coverage and did not preserve the specialist-review
  record beside the evidence.

## Corrections and re-review

- Trails now plot recorded points against elapsed time and a local value window, have no decorative
  data, show Older → Now, retain visible contrast in light mode, and record manual/reset changes in
  both the per-emotion path and readable history.
- CONNECT begins empty. Discovery is labeled Sample in the mock, checked accounts determine the
  result, and account state survives reload.
- The off-MIND presence control is a 54px corner presence button rather than a central strip.
- Automation summaries derive from cycle/time, rows support Enter/Space, delivery choices are unique,
  and a lightweight inline History/validation view is present.
- Faint tokens exceed 4.5:1, all five Feeling levels wrap on phone widths, Feelings-off disables
  editing, and Inner state stays consistent across CHARACTER and MIND.
- QA now exercises the complete light and dark width matrix, phone console, stop path, account
  selection and reload, trail travel, manual history, keyboard automation, and visible mobile ranges.
