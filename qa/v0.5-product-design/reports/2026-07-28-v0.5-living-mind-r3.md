# Viventium V0.5 Living Mind — R3 Design QA

> **Result:** local prototype acceptance PASS; independent Visual, Interaction/Accessibility, and
> Viventium Product-Truth reviewers all returned PASS with no remaining P0, P1, or P2 findings.
> R3 remains a review artifact, not an owner-approved design or V0.5 runtime.

## Why R3 exists

R2 did not meet the intended visual or product truth. Its per-emotion charts auto-scaled movement,
used stretched decorative geometry, and made CHARACTER feel like an administrative page instead of
the approved live Feelings instrument. MIND also lacked an honest, useful way to inspect the Memory
and Cortex work behind a consequential reply.

R3 keeps the approved quiet-graphite shell and replaces those weak surfaces.

## Product corrections

- MIND carries one collapsed-by-default **Behind this reply** line under the response it belongs to.
  Its detail answers: what context Viventium was given, what specialists returned, and what changed.
- Life sources, Conversation Recall, and saved Memory are named separately. The example shows a
  useful Cortex contribution, a partially unavailable source, active work, interruption/retry, a
  silent check, and an inferred Life update that still requires review.
- CHARACTER now follows the live Feelings visual grammar: nine fixed 0–100 vertical bands, Current
  fill/cap, Nature marker, recorded path, one focused inspector, Inner state, Nature profiles,
  reaction controls, and the readable reaction trail.
- The visual paths and readable history derive from the same canonical typed event shape:
  timestamp, band, direction, `slight|clear|strong`, allowed cause, source type, before, and after.
- The embodied capsule opening sentence is verbatim canonical. Its raw payload sits behind
  **Advanced** in consumer UI and is canonically empty while Feelings are paused or no band is
  Felt. Personal additions are normalized, capped, persisted, and erased with the rest of the
  Feelings/Cortex state.
- On phones, the compact presence control lives in app chrome rather than over the composer or
  Feeling controls. Selecting a Feeling brings its focused controls into view.

## Visual review performed

The produced desktop and phone screenshots were opened at original resolution and inspected after
the automated run. This caught issues that source inspection did not: the former stretched Feeling
markers, full voice island obscuring MIND evidence, compact presence obscuring phone content, a
large phone dead zone between conversation and Emotional State, raw developer capsule dominating
CHARACTER, and fixed/sticky screenshot artifacts. Browser interaction then caught missing custom-
instruction refresh and switch focus styling. The current evidence set was regenerated after those
corrections.

## Honest boundaries

- No account, source, browser profile, provider, or credential was accessed.
- GlassHive discovery, Cortex results, Memory/Life updates, voice, and automation execution are
  interactive simulations only.
- No V0.5 runtime or production persistence is claimed.
- Fresh authenticated `/feelings` runtime parity could not be rerun in this pass. R3 was grounded in
  the canonical requirement, current component source, and existing approved desktop/mobile visual
  evidence; fresh runtime parity remains a future implementation gate.
- R3 is not owner-approved until the product owner explicitly accepts it.
