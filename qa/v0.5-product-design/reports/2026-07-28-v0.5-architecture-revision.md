## Result

**PASS for product-owner review; not approved product truth.** The revised system map now presents
Viventium as one user-owned continuity and compounding loop rather than a flat inventory of product
features or a LibreChat-centered stack.

## Escaped Review Failure

The prior architecture was visually clean but failed two product-owner review tests:

1. it did not directly prove why or how Viventium should exist after frontier agent products became
   substantially more capable; and
2. it did not group the system into a clear hierarchy or connect its parts as a single-glass-pane
   view.

The root cause was architectural framing: components and ownership layers were documented before the
product's compounding person-level loop was made the organizing spine.

## Corrected Product Answer

Viventium is proposed as the neutral, local, user-owned continuity system across replaceable AI
bodies. It should not compete as another chat UI, model switcher, connector marketplace, prompt
library, or reskinned harness. It owns the person-specific custody and compounding loop:

`sense → remember → understand → feel → think → act → learn → return`

The architecture now distinguishes:

- the person's authoritative world outside Viventium;
- replaceable frontier intelligence and native integrations outside Viventium;
- four user-facing control doors inside Viventium;
- the portable Life and Brain Pack assets;
- live cognition, delegation, compounding, trust, and local runtime state; and
- the explicit return from overnight work to the person's next interaction.
