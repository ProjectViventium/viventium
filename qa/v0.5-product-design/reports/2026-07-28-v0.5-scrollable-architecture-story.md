## Information-Architecture Correction

The architecture review exposed four real omissions in the public-safe Life fixture. `Calendar/`,
`Social/`, `Places/`, and `Events/` are now explicit canonical roots with their own operating notes.
This prevents calendar evidence from being forced into Plans, social evidence from becoming an
ambiguous communication or media bucket, and places or lived experiences from being incorrectly
treated as media types.

Files and Notes remain source formats rather than invented life domains. Their accepted content is
routed by meaning; their source manifests stay under `Life/Sources/`. Raw downloads wait under
`Life/99_System/intake/` until classified.

## Defects Found And Corrected During QA

- Removed scroll-reveal opacity because off-screen sections were invisible in a complete page
  capture and would violate the no-hidden-layers requirement if JavaScript or observation failed.
- Reduced the mobile display scale so `Everywhere.` retains the page gutter at 390px.
- Replaced the old compressed World grouping with one source per row.
- Added explicit `Calendar/` and `Social/` roots to the Life fixture and its agent routing rules.
- Promoted `Places/` and `Events/` out of Media into their own canonical roots.
- Clarified that `Communications/Listen_Only/`, `Wing_Mode/`, and `Transcripts/` are views or indexes;
  canonical ambient and transcript evidence remains under `Memory/`.
- Added an explicit Return-to-Sense cycle and clarified that product doors are overlapping control
  surfaces rather than exclusive owners of system stages.
- Restored the Authority field at tablet width and changed the source crosswalk to a legible stacked
  layout there.
