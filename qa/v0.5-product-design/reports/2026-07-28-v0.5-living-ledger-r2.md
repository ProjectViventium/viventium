# Viventium V0.5 Living Ledger — R2 Design QA

> **Result:** PASS as a production-shaped review artifact. It is not an approved design or a
> functional V0.5 runtime.

## Why R2 exists

The quiet-graphite R1 candidate improved visual restraint but failed important product truths: it
treated channels as alternate selves, made connection setup too manual, assumed one account per
service, omitted the temporal journey of each Feeling, exposed internal terminology, and separated
Automations from the canonical Prompt Workbench interaction.

R1 is preserved unchanged under
`docs/research_and_future_plans/v0.5/artifacts/revisions/quiet-graphite-r1/`.

## Evidence used before design

- Canonical Feelings requirements, band definitions, typed reactions, return behavior, and the live
  `/feelings` component source were inspected. R2 uses the exact nine bands, poles, words, Nature
  values, and valid `3/8/15` typed movement strengths.
- The current Prompt Workbench scheduled-prompt surface and its real `{{` variable registry were
  inspected. R2 exposes those same concepts through a lightweight inline view.
- Three fresh-context reviews were completed: visual/UI quality, interaction and current-practice
  research, and Viventium project/source-of-truth accuracy.
- Gemini Pro Extended Deep Research independently reviewed the discovery, multi-account,
  emotional-history, automation, voice-presence, accessibility, and lightweight implementation
  patterns. Its recommendations were treated as a research input, not product truth.
- The proposal was cross-checked against primary guidance from Apple HIG, WCAG 2.2, WAI-ARIA,
  official OpenAI/Anthropic computer-use documentation, Google OAuth guidance, Tauri security, and
  current first-party product patterns from Linear and Todoist.

Those first specialist reviews did not catch every defect. A later Claude Opus 5 adversarial pass
rejected the first R2 build, most importantly because its emotional traces visually flattened real
movement and included decorative movement. It also found discovery-state, persistence, keyboard,
contrast, mobile, automation-history, and QA-overclaim defects. All verified blockers were repaired;
Claude's corrected-tree follow-up verdict was **PASS FOR OWNER REVIEW**. The full, intentionally
unflattering review record is kept under `../reviews/`.

## What changed

- MIND is one conversation with quiet source provenance, current Emotional State, and a compact
  Emotion Trail. The Here/Telegram switch is gone.
- CONNECT leads with a GlassHive-powered **Find everything** action. Discovery is visible,
  interruptible, and read-only until the person reviews accounts and selects **Connect selected**.
- Every service can contain several named accounts with independent controls and **Add another**.
- CHARACTER uses the canonical Feelings model and includes a visible temporal trail for every
  emotion, followed by human-facing controls: Current, Natural, How quickly it returns, and Felt.
- AUTOMATIONS expands cycle, instruction, context, delivery, latest result, and history inline. Its
  prompt uses the canonical Prompt Workbench `{{` variables and autocomplete.
- The voice-presence dock keeps its complete listening controls in MIND and compacts on task-heavy
  pages so it does not cover the work.

## What was not run

- No provider account was connected and no authorization was requested.
- GlassHive discovery is a truthful interaction simulation; it did not inspect the QA machine.
- Voice did not open a real LiveKit session, listen, speak, or change microphone state.
- Automation Save changed mock local state only and did not write Prompt Workbench or execute a job.
- No V0.5 runtime, connector, memory import, Life sync, or production account persistence is
  claimed. The prototype alone persists its synthetic review state locally.

These are intentional prototype boundaries. Each becomes a separate runtime acceptance gate if the
product owner approves R2 for implementation.

## Remaining decision

R2 has passed the local interaction matrix and the corrected-tree adversarial review. It is ready
for owner evaluation, not owner-approved. The next action is approval, rejection, or a short list
of focused changes; implementation should not begin before that decision.

R1 is preserved locally in the revision folder but the V0.5 artifact tree is not yet committed, so
that preservation is not durable source control. No staging or commit was performed without owner
authorization.
